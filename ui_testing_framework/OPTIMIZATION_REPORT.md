## 🎯 **Optimizations Implemented**

### **1. Verbose Opening Elimination**
**Pattern:** Philosophical introductions like "Let us embark on a journey..."
**Solution:** Direct action statements
**Savings:** 10-15 tokens per strategy

### **2. Step Header Compression**
**Pattern:** `**STEP 1: DECOMPOSITION** (Reductionist Phase)`
**Solution:** `**STEP 1: DECOMPOSE**`
**Savings:** 5-8 tokens per step

### **3. Bullet Point Streamlining**
**Pattern:** "What are the irreducible elements?"
**Solution:** "Elements, relationships, principles, patterns"
**Savings:** 3-5 tokens per bullet point

### **4. Content Consolidation**
**Pattern:** Repetitive structural explanations
**Solution:** Compressed process descriptions
**Savings:** 20-30 tokens per strategy

### **5. Format Optimization**
**Pattern:** Excessive ASCII formatting and separators
**Solution:** Clean, minimal formatting
**Savings:** 10-15 tokens per strategy
