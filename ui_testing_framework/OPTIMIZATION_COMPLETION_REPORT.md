## 🎨 **Optimization Techniques Applied**

### **1. Verbose Opening Elimination**
**Before:** "Let us embark on a journey of reasoning that honors the fundamental principles..."  
**After:** "Apply systematic reasoning:"  
**Savings:** 10-15 tokens per strategy

### **2. Step Header Compression**
**Before:** `**STEP 1: DECOMPOSITION** (Reductionist Phase)`  
**After:** `**DECOMPOSE**`  
**Savings:** 5-8 tokens per step

### **3. Bullet Point Streamlining**
**Before:** "What are the irreducible elements that compose this system?"  
**After:** "Elements, relationships, principles, patterns"  
**Savings:** 3-5 tokens per bullet

### **4. Content Consolidation**
**Before:** Detailed explanations with examples and sub-explanations  
**After:** Core concepts with essential information only  
**Savings:** 20-30 tokens per strategy

### **5. Format Optimization**
**Before:** Excessive ASCII formatting and decorative separators  
**After:** Clean, minimal formatting preserving readability  
**Savings:** 10-15 tokens per strategy
